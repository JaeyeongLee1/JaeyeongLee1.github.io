Bayesian Deep Learning · Bayesian Nonparametrics · Spatio-Temporal Modeling

Hi, I’m Jaeyeong Lee.

My research focuses on Bayesian modeling and inference for complex functions, distributions, and stochastic systems, with particular interests in Bayesian deep learning, Bayesian nonparametrics, and spatio-temporal modeling.

I am particularly interested in flexible prior design, principled inference, and the statistical behavior of posterior distributions, as well as applications to real-world industrial and engineering systems.
