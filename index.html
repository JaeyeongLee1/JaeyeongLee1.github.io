Trustworthy AI · Uncertainty · Time Series

Hello, I’m Jaeyeong Lee.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial & Systems Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Heeyoung Kim. My research focuses on trustworthy AI and dynamical systems, with particular interests in Bayesian deep learning, uncertainty quantification, probabilistic modeling, and learning from complex temporal and spatio-temporal data.

I am interested in developing principled machine learning methods that remain reliable under uncertainty, temporal dependence, irregular observations, and distributional change, especially for scientific and industrial dynamical systems.
