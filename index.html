Statistical AI · Uncertainty · Time Series

Hello, I’m Jaeyeong Lee.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Industrial & Systems Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Heeyoung Kim. My research lies at the intersection of statistical machine learning and artificial intelligence, with a particular interest in uncertainty quantification and learning from complex temporal and spatio-temporal data.

I am interested in developing principled machine learning methods that remain reliable under uncertainty, temporal dependence, irregular observations, and distributional change, especially for scientific and industrial dynamical systems.

Research Interest

What I work on

Uncertainty Quantification

Bayesian inference, probabilistic prediction, calibration, function-space modeling, and trustworthy decision-making under uncertainty.

Time Series & Dynamical Systems

Temporal dependence, irregular and continuous-time data, spatio-temporal dynamics, forecasting, and anomaly detection.

Statistical Machine Learning

Statistically principled learning methods for structured, scientific, and industrial data.
