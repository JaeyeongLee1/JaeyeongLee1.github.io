Statistics with depth.

We develop data science methodologies for complex real-world systems, bringing together statistical thinking, modern artificial intelligence, and the discipline of industrial engineering.

Statistics×Artificial Intelligence×Industrial Systems

A research tradition in three languages

One laboratory, three intellectual homes.

Our work is united by a simple conviction: difficult engineering problems deserve methods that are not only useful, but also statistically principled and intellectually durable.

The laboratory moves comfortably between Bayesian modeling, uncertainty and causal reasoning, modern deep learning, time-series and spatio-temporal analysis, and quality-oriented industrial applications.

I

Statistical Foundations

Bayesian modeling, nonparametrics, spatial and spatio-temporal statistics, uncertainty quantification, causal inference, and principled learning under incomplete information.

II

Artificial Intelligence

Deep generative models, evidential learning, semi-supervised learning, domain generalization, representation learning, anomaly detection, and robust predictive systems.

III

Industrial Intelligence

Methods shaped by real systems: manufacturing quality, multistage processes, semiconductor analytics, time-series monitoring, optimization, and engineering decision support.
